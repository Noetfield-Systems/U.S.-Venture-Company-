FOUNDERS ASK

Answers before
you get started.

Can I start a U.S. company if I live outside the United States?+

Often, yes. Ownership eligibility and tax obligations depend on the entity type, your residence and actual business activity.

Should I choose a C-Corporation or LLC?+

Venture-backed startups often use a Delaware C-Corp. Other digital businesses may consider an LLC. Your personal tax and operating situation matter, so seek appropriate professional advice before filing.

Does this website register companies today?+

Not yet. This is an early-access formation and market-entry planning platform. Official filing services will be offered only after qualified filing arrangements and a lawful contracting entity are in place.

Can a U.S. company guarantee banking or investors?+

No. Banking, investment, tax, immigration and government funding decisions are made independently, and incorporation does not guarantee them.

Do I need to rent an office in the U.S.?+

Not necessarily. A registered agent and physical operating presence serve different functions, and tax/registration obligations depend on actual activity in each state.
