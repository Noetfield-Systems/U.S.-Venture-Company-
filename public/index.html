Do I need a U.S. corporation to work with you?+

Not necessarily. We start with your market-entry goal. An independent qualified professional can advise if a legal entity is appropriate.

Can you guarantee investors or a U.S. bank account?+

No. We do not guarantee financing, customer acquisition, banking approval, or government program eligibility.

Is the company already incorporated?+

Not yet. This is an early-access market validation site for a proposed U.S. venture. Any legal entity status will be updated only after confirmed registration.

What do AI systems actually do?+

They may support research, coordination, and workflow preparation. Client-facing financial, contractual, and other consequential actions remain subject to authorized human review.
