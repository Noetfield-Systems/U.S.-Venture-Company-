# Actual Formation-Category UX System v2
**Design benchmark:** Stripe Atlas first; Clerky, Firstbase and doola second. These are direct competitors; abstract B2B visual inspiration is not admissible as the spec.

## Design direction
Bright near-white background, crisp dark text, restrained violet/cobalt brand accent, subtle lilac/cool gradient, precise sans-serif type. UX is a premium infrastructure website, not luxury-gold finance or dramatic scenery. No New York skyline, Golden Gate, global-globe map, rocket, fake partner/client logos, synthetic certificates, fake progress metrics or invented agent profiles.

Reference layout:
1. Simple header: brand | Why us / How it works / C-Corp / LLC / Pricing / Resources | main "Plan your company" CTA. Omit Sign in unless actual customer auth exists.
2. Hero: eyebrow "U.S. company formation for global founders"; H1 "Launch your U.S. company. Build what comes next."; copy describing actual current planning and planned filing/operating services; primary CTA to real readiness wizard; secondary CTA steps.
3. Hero product preview: realistic browser frame with entity choice "C-Corporation / LLC / Not sure", cofounder count, 4-stage formation checklist. Every "complete" state must be backed by real data; mockup has neutral "Preview" labels.
4. Four buyer jobs: Choose structure / Incorporate / Set up equity & EIN / Stay operational.
5. Transparent costs: price including and excluding state/agent, renewal examples, no unexpected recurring costs.
6. Four steps: Plan → File → Set up → Operate. Any missing feature labeled upcoming.
7. Compare C-Corp vs LLC, at decision level without fake individualized tax advice.
8. FAQ for non-US founders, ownership, EIN, bank approvals, state nexus, time limits, annual renewals.
9. Specific transparent footer: founding-stage project / company not yet formed / real support contact and privacy.

## Product-style rules from direct competitors
Atlas: authentic documentation concept, under-10-minute self-serve choice path, one obvious CTA, brief explanations.
Clerky: exact document names and sequence: charter, board actions, stock purchase, 83(b), SAFE, cap table. No vague "legally ready" badges.
Firstbase: clear recurring bundles and renewal prices.
doola: international founder plain-language onboarding, founder roles and support plans.
Gust: status of stock grants, option pool, SAFE issuance and actual document workflow.
StartGlobal: full price and renewals.

## Wizard
Step 1 goal: funding/venture-backed startup, solo business, unsure.
Step 2 entity: C-Corp, LLC, unsure.
Step 3 founders: number, country of residence (NOT passport or SSN in public form).
Step 4 anticipated operations: state, remote-only or in-state work.
Step 5 timeline + company already formed? + contact consent.
Output: real saved plan/inquiry ID, next step, educational structure comparison and estimated costs clearly sourced. No charge until service is truly offered.

## User portal planned
Case overview, founders/equity, state filing, EIN, documents/signatures, banking steps, annual obligations, support. Every legal/financial status must have timestamp, human/provider actor, evidence link and verifiability.

## Mobile/accessibility
360, 390, 768, 1024, 1440px breakpoints, keyboard accessibility, focus states, AA contrast, reduced motion, visual density appropriate to actual UI. No empty video CTAs, fake "trusted" strips or unverifiable progress percentages.
