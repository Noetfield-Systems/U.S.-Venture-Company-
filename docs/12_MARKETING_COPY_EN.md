## FAQ
**Can non-US citizens be shareholders?** Yes in many US corporation situations; tax and ownership limitations can vary.
**Is Delaware required?** No, it is often favored by venture startups but the real operating state's rules also matter.
**Can I open a U.S. bank account?** You may apply through independent providers; approval is not guaranteed.
**Can you incorporate my company today?** Not yet. Currently this is a planning and market-validation platform.
**Can this help get investment?** Corporate structure may facilitate standard share financing; no investors or funding are guaranteed.
**Is this company already incorporated?** No, the US venture itself is a founding-stage project pending actual filing.
