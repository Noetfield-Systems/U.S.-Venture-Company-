# Interactive product demo v4 — production implementation, sample data
**13 November 2026**

## Product experience
Public landing hero and platform discovery now lead to /demo, a complete eight-area self-guided application concept. Every item is interactive, with locally persisted sample choices, export, reset, and navigable pages.

## Sample workspace modules
- **Overview:** sample launch timeline, editable milestones, sample completion metrics and activity
- **Formation:** C-Corp / LLC, jurisdiction, number of founders, funding strategy, itemized illustrative fees, typical annual state costs
- **Founders & equity:** editable founder names, allocation slider, cap table, downloadable sample ownership memo
- **Documents:** eight document workflows, status review, explanatory modal and source/evidence design
- **EIN & banking:** status stages, preparation checklist and banking document planning
- **Operations:** annual state tax/report reminders, agent and tax checklists
- **Market entry:** ICP and offer inputs, editable local sample pipeline and stage transitions
- **Fundraising:** SAFE, cap table, financials, diligence checklist and simulated data room

## Truthful customer boundary without disabling exploration
Screens are *interactive demonstrations* with clear "sample" context, not literal claims that an authorized public state filing or bank action took place. They should nevertheless be comprehensive, beautiful and useful for selling the future product. The real public assessment remains live via /#assessment. This is not a blocked or inert experience.

## Direct benchmark integrations
- Stripe Atlas: jurisdiction and entity choice, documentation, EIN, shares and funding sequence.
- Clerky: post-incorporation documents, stock workflow and partner API that can provide a real Delaware C-Corp filing path (https://developers.clerky.com/).
- Firstbase: operational recurring services, agent, tax, mail and banking.
Use actual partner credentials/agreements when authorized; never use fake filing receipts or invented connected providers.
