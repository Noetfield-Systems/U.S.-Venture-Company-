# MASTER BLUEPRINT — U.S. Venture Company
Version 1.1 | 2026-11-13 | founder-stage implementation document

## Mission
Enable international software/AI founders to enter and test the U.S. market with accountable, affordable execution. We compete on **delivery after the initial market decision**, not on making formation paperwork appear magically free.

## Legal and operating thesis
Proposed legal structure: Delaware C-Corporation. Founding ownership: Canada-based non-executive investor/shareholder and California-based U.S. founding partner; no percentages allocated or roles legally accepted. Future cofounders/partners anywhere in the world. Noetfield Systems Inc. is a separate prospective service provider that may deliver technical and operational services via signed MSA/SOW. No fictitious CA/Delaware physical office. Registered agent is for legal service of process, not a public customer-facing office.

Pre-incorporation constraint: site is a market validation and expression-of-interest channel only. Do not represent formation, securities offering, current financing, paid customers, compliance certification, or bank access as existing facts.

## Commercial wedge
Offer free initial founder assessment, pilot $249 readiness brief, indicative $1,490 two-week U.S. launch sprint, and bounded $499/month execution desk. Any paid work requires an appropriate actual contracting entity, customer-approved scope, verified payment processing and transparent terms. Product operations should support but not substitute professional legal, tax or securities services.

## Value proposition
1. Buyer-specific U.S. market clarity rather than generic advice.
2. Defined scope, named owner, due date, QA, proof, handoff.
3. Technical leverage of AI-assisted execution without claiming 100 human employees.
4. Global remote collaboration.
5. Expandable Delaware equity / governance model attractive to conventional venture finance.

## Competitive landscape
Stripe Atlas: exceptional incorporation UX, startup banking/payments ecosystem. Clerky: financing-standard founder paperwork. Firstbase: broader business operations. Our opportunity is not copying a state filing service; it is rapid, documented go-to-market and managed execution. Do not falsely assert endorsed partnerships.

## Go/no-go signals
Week 1: live page, real durable lead writes, 10 targeted founder conversations, 3 scoped proposals. By day 30: validate real willingness to pay; 3 paying customers a stretch hypothesis, never a claim. Day 90: 10 cumulative customers stretch hypothesis and retention/contribution-margin evidence. Pivot the ICP/service offer if cost to acquire and delivery margin are unattractive.

## Scoreboard
Visitors; qualified inquiries; contact-to-discovery%; proposal%; actual settled revenue; COGS; gross contribution; delivery acceptance%; refunds; actual buyer outcomes. All actual/target/demo states separated.

## Responsibility and authority
U.S. corporation only after formation may execute contracts and bank/merchant functions through authorized human officers. Canada shareholder governance is nonexecutive; Noetfield provides only scoped services. Material capital, payments, regulated advice and legal decisions remain with authorized natural persons.

## Documents to complete before signing
Founder MOU, confirmed cap table and vesting, incorporator documents, board actions, bylaws, stock purchase/83(b) advice if applicable, IP assignment, vendor MSA/SOW, customer terms, privacy, banking/KYC and regulatory/tax review. All template text here is draft—not filed or legal advice.
