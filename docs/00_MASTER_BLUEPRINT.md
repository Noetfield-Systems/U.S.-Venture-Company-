# U.S. Venture Company — Master Blueprint v2.0
Date: 2026-11-13. Founding-stage design. Not a filed corporation.

## Mission and product category
Build a US incorporation and startup-operating platform for international founders. Primary benchmark: Stripe Atlas. Secondary **direct category** competitors: Clerky, Firstbase, doola, Gust Launch, StartGlobal, Commenda. Do not use Linear, Attio, Vanta, Ramp, generic CRM, banking or abstract design references as the primary product benchmark.

**Single-sentence promise:** Form your U.S. company, get founder setup in order, and manage the essentials that come next.

## Real customer journey
Choose LLC or Delaware C-Corp → verify eligibility and intended state of operation → see itemized price and annual obligations → enter founder/team details → invite cofounders → e-sign authorized documents → file through genuine qualified provider → retrieve official filing evidence → complete founder equity/83(b) where relevant → apply for EIN → coordinate bank/payment applications → manage taxes, annual reports, registered-agent renewals → optionally obtain operating and customer-launch support.

This is the **target architecture**, not a representation of current product functionality. As of today only the public founding-stage site, intake API, and private operator lead queue exist. No real filing engine, company EIN, signed founder cap table, merchant checkout or banking integration has been verified.

## Differentiation that does not deny competitor features
Atlas: outstanding incorporation flow, equity, 83(b), EIN, banking/payment and SAFE readiness. We should benchmark these real features instead of pretending Atlas is a paperwork-only product.
Clerky: startup documentation depth and investor correctness. Firstbase: formation plus back-office subscriptions. doola: international formation + tax/bookkeeping. Gust: cap table and finance workflow. StartGlobal: clear total-cost explanation. Commenda: case progress and international compliance calendar.

Our hypothesis: a founder sees the *whole entity lifecycle* in one workspace, with a transparent total-cost forecast, genuine filing receipts, cofounder collaboration, and optional bounded real-world operational execution via vetted providers. Validate the hypothesis through actual buyers; never claim unique features without comparison evidence.

## ICP priority
P0: 1–4 international software/AI cofounders wanting venture-funded Delaware C-Corp; urgent ownership, equity, EIN, investor-readiness needs.
P1: foreign-resident digital entrepreneurs / ecommerce operators seeking US LLC and predictable compliance expenses.
P1: already formed startups seeking recurring maintenance and document/equity workflows.
P2: complex foreign business expansion into multiple states (only after specialist provider capacity).

## Product surfaces
1. Marketing site: crisp formation-first hero, comparison of C-Corp/LLC, direct primary CTA, step sequence, cost breakdown, FAQ, actual legal identity.
2. Readiness planner: short structured form, founder role, jurisdiction, funding intent, owners, timeline, quote preview, consent and reference ID.
3. Formation workspace (future): case timeline, founder invites, authorized provider and filing status, documents/signatures, state evidence.
4. Post-formation operations (future): EIN, bank guidance, obligations calendar, support, entity documents, recurring services.
5. Operator console: current private prospect queue; future authorized case dispatch, QA, financial reconciliation and audit receipt.

Canonical entities: founder, company_case, entity_selection, founder_invite, ownership_intent, quote, order, service_provider, filing_case, filing_receipt, signature, stock_issuance, ein_case, bank_application, compliance_obligation, support_case, workpacket, verified_receipt. Every state transition requires correct actor, timestamp and provenance.

## Commercial model
Free Formation Readiness (live inquiry only). Prospective Core Formation coordination ($499 service-price hypothesis plus correctly disclosed actual state/agent/provider costs), Founder Documents & Equity add-on, Annual Essentials membership, optional paid Operations Desk and US GTM Sprint.
**Never market filing/checkout as live until an actual contracting company, authorized filing provider, merchant, pricing, refund terms and documentary path exist.** Existing Noetfield may contract for actual independent technical/market research services under its own identity, but must not pose as a filed US corporation.

## Real shareholder/operator boundary
Proposed independent Delaware C-Corp; Canada-based shareholder/investor has no executive title unless expressly agreed; California-based founding shareholder may accept legal director/officer duties, never nominally; legally appointed natural-person officers/director must perform real duties. Noetfield Systems Inc. is a separate Canadian technology vendor via written MSA/SOW with scope, data transfer boundaries and fees. None of these roles is filed or accepted as of this document.
Delaware formation and CA business activity are separately evaluated; online-only is not a universal tax exemption. US shares do not grant immigration status, loans, funding or bank approval.

## GTM, validation and proof
Prioritize formation-intent SEO, founder communities, founder referrals, comparisons with official dated data, and transparent costs. Score actual qualified completion, quote acceptance, paid confirmed orders, filings verified by official receipt, customer satisfaction, renewal and contribution margin. No fictitious customers/traction.

## Release criteria
A real paid filing must pass signed scope, correct entity and merchant identity, authorized human instruction, known official fees, provider acknowledgment, state-issued confirmation, recorded audit trail and delivery receipt. A concept dashboard or submitted assessment never counts as an incorporation.
