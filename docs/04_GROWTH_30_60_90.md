# Formation-first GTM and 90-day execution v2
## Day 0 — factual baseline
Existing site and API published on Railway with a single-replica persistent queue. Current brand is working name; Delaware company not yet incorporated. No actual customer incorporation or collected payment is evidenced.

## Days 1–14 — narrow category validation
- Replace vague market-entry branding with formation-first Atlas-category homepage and functional structure-intent step.
- Conduct 15 interviews with non-US C-Corp-seeking teams, 10 with LLC-seeking operators.
- Publish neutral source-dated articles on total cost, LLC vs C-Corp, Delaware vs California activity, founder equity/EIN/83(b) process.
- Collect 30 targeted consented founders; qualify at least 10; submit 3 explicit offers that distinguish current assessment from prospective filing.
- Obtain provider quotes/requirements for registered agent, filer, EIN and stock document workflow; no fake partnerships.

## Days 15–30 — revenue mechanism
- Establish real entity or disclose any existing authorized contracting company to sell permitted service.
- Turn on actual checkout only after contracting/refund/provider costs known.
- Goal hypothesis: 3 paid verified orders, not a retrospective claim. For each, compute gross contribution and true provider cost.
- Build cofounder-invitation, case-state and quote-tracking modules with authenticated user access.

## Days 31–60 — deliver real formation
- Carry a test customer through genuine filing, obtain state receipt and approved documents; attach no fake official certificates.
- Build EIN and recurring tax/compliance reminders based on correct trigger.
- Measure order-to-file elapsed time and exceptions by country of founders.
- Publish only consented case studies and verified success outcomes.
- Begin annual support upsell if actually deliverable.

## Days 61–90 — improve and scale
- Repeatable formation engine, full audit trail, truthful price, no manual mystery states.
- Target hypothesis: 10 cumulative successful legitimate paid orders; >40% true contribution margin after provider costs; no claim before actual data.
- Optimize onboarding and price transparency against the same primary direct competitors.
- Raise private/angel capital only using genuine clients, contracts, income, cap table and official corporate status.

## Metrics tree
Qualified intent by entity type; planning completion; quote view/acceptance; signed order; payment actually settled; filing initiated; state accepted; EIN delivered; documents completed; annual support attach/retention; refund/dispute; customer NPS; contribution profit. Show current vs estimated vs simulated separately.

## Acquisition channels
SEO for explicit formation queries, founder accelerator groups, targeted professional communities, opt-in referral partners, education about hidden renewals. Avoid buying traffic to non-functioning official filing claims.
