# 30 / 60 / 90 DAY LAUNCH SCORECARD
Day 0: deploy site, consented intake, operator desk, health endpoint; disclose stage.
Days 1–14: 30 targeted founder messages in relevant communities/network, 10 quality discovery calls, 3 written offers with defined margin. Validate actual painful workflow; do not mass spam.
Days 15–30: target 3 paying pilot customers after lawful merchant entity is ready; baseline CAC, labor hours and delivered QA acceptance. If payment cannot start, count only qualified interest, not revenue.
Days 31–60: deliver first repeatable sprint, document proof receipts with client consent, create standardized SOW/templates, set followup cadence, refine pricing.
Days 61–90: target 10 cumulative pilot customers as stretch hypothesis; 2+ renewal signals, >50% scoped gross contribution target, low refund rate, referral mechanism and investor-ready traction dossier.
Kill/pivot trigger: unresolved customer urgency, unprofitable delivery economics after 5 pilots, or inability to demonstrate verifiable customer value.
