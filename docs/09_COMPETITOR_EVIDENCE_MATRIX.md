## Evidence-based gaps to test — not claims
Do founders need better cofounder orchestration, transparent annual-total-cost, a shared obligations calendar, quicker support, operational follow-through, or explicit filing receipt verification? Conduct 20–30 discovery interviews and measure need instead of asserting Atlas lacks those features.
